Team project, still in progress. The cover shows an illustrated interface with example scores, not real results.

01 · Goal

Interview practice that explains its scores

Candidates practise interview questions and get feedback they can act on. Every score comes from clear rules, and the app says what to work on next.

02 · Constraint

No paid AI service required

The budget is zero, so the backend works without any paid or external AI service by default. A rule-based engine does the scoring. An LLM can be switched on, but it's off unless configured, local-first, and policy-checked.

03 · What I built

Backend features, milestone by milestone

  • 01
    Accounts and securityJWT authentication with refresh-token rotation.
  • 02
    CV upload and parsingReads PDF and DOCX CVs and extracts a candidate profile, tracking where each fact came from (extracted, inferred or added by the user).
  • 03
    Question bankA curated bank of 71 questions with reference answers.
  • 04
    Answer evaluationA six-dimension rubric: relevance, key concepts, technical accuracy, structure, evidence and communication. It deliberately doesn't claim to measure "confidence".
  • 05
    Adaptive follow-upsFollow-up questions chosen by a policy-validated agent.
  • 06
    Improvement planSeparates a single weak answer from a confirmed skill gap.
04 · Way of working

Verify before moving on

Each milestone was confirmed working locally before the next one started, and the backend is covered by 272 automated tests. The data lives in PostgreSQL, with SQLAlchemy models and Alembic migrations.
