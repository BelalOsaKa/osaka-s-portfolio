01 · Scope

One site for eight sections

The department has eight sections: Investments, Marketing, Banking, Petroleum Facilities Management, HR, MIS, Economics and Accounting. Each one needs its students, their grades and a daily schedule in one place.

02 · The plan

Three parts, one data model

  • 01
    Student recordsStudents per section, starting with a synthetic dataset while the site is built.
  • 02
    GradesPer course and per student, organised before they're shown.
  • 03
    Daily schedulesOne for each of the eight sections.
  • 04
    A small ML modelPlanned, to organise the data before it appears on the site.
