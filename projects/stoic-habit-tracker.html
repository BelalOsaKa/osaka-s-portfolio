Stoic HabitTracker

Habit apps on phones are full of distractions. I wanted a simple desktop tool that shows a whole month at once, so gaps and streaks are obvious.

01 · What it does

Every habit, every day of the month, in one view

  • 01
    Habits and categoriesAdd, edit and delete habits, and group them into categories.
  • 02
    Daily check-insTick each day in a monthly grid and move between months. The app creates each month's days automatically.
  • 03
    ProgressCompletion rate and longest streak for every habit, a monthly summary of your best and worst days, a GitHub-style heatmap and Matplotlib progress charts.
  • 04
    Your data, your wayDark and light themes, CSV export of any month, and optional desktop notifications.
02 · How it's built

Clean layers

A PySide6 interface talks to a controller layer that holds all the logic. SQLAlchemy models (Habit and HabitDay) store everything in a SQLite file, and deleting a habit removes its days too.

03 · Shipping it

A real Windows app, not just a script

I packaged it with PyInstaller into a standalone .exe and wrote build scripts plus an installer that adds desktop and Start-menu shortcuts. The database sits next to the .exe, so data persists between runs.
